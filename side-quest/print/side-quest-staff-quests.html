1. Look at their screen. It must say Quest complete!, with a moving background and a light sweeping over the trophy. A still image = a screenshot.
2. Check the quest on their screen matches this list and the proof is what the right-hand column says.
3. Judge by eye. If it looks done in good faith, give the prize. No codes, no stamps.
4. The time under "Completed at" is when they pressed "I did it". One swap per phone; "Play again" exists, so watch for repeat visits if prizes run low.
